## Town Center

The 2023 tree has three Town Center buildings. Per-civ ages from `civilization_object`:

| Tree id | internal_name | Build time (s) | Per-civ age | Log names mapped to it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 109 | RTWC | 100 | age 1: 45 civs | CREATE_INITIAL `Town Center` |
| 621 | RTWC1X | 150 | age 2: 1 civs (manual: Cumans); age 3: 44 civs | BUILD `Town Center` |
| 71 | RTWC2 | 100 | no civ has it | - |

109 is the Town Center of the Dark Age lane in the 2023 `js/techtree.js` (`TOWN_CENTER`), the TC a player starts with. 621 (`TOWN_CENTER_2`) is the additional TC in the Castle Age lane. Building a TC maps to 621, so plausibility checks must use the per-civ age from `civilization_object` (Cumans: Feudal). Exception: on maps without a starting TC the first TC is built in the Dark Age.

**Population:** neither the 2023 nor the 2026 data.json has population cost or provided population. No `pop_cost` or `pop_provided` columns were added; this is a known limitation.

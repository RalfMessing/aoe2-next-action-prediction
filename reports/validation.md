## Known limitations

- START and COMPLETE times are simulated by the dataset authors from a fixed duration per type (0 s for many). Only COMMAND (and START for buildings) are real decision times.
- Research durations in the log mostly match no rule version; COMPLETE RESEARCH times are unusable.
- Age-up commands are missing in many sessions that clearly reach the age; the age estimate falls back to the first use of an age-gated object.
- Celts, Dravidians, Hindustanis (also Romans, Georgians) have no unique unit or tech in the log; their events are probably mislabeled or dropped, so these civs are rarely or never identified.
- Replacement buildings are logged under base names (Folwark as Mill, Fortified Church as Monastery); handled via data/manual/replacement_equivalents.csv. Mule Cart is not modeled.
- Not in the log: civ, map, Elo, match outcome, attacks, deaths, gathered amounts, cancelled production. Villager counts are 'trained so far'; spending can be overestimated.
- Rules are the 2023 tree without civ cost bonuses and without population data. Age-up building rules come from general game knowledge (data/manual/manual_dependencies.csv); Folwark and Fortified Church counting toward age-ups is an assumption; Mule Cart, Krepost and Donjon are not modeled as age-up buildings.
- The spending check is a loose sanity bound (0.5 resources per villager-second), not an income model.
- The 2023 tree lists the Xolotl Warrior for Aztecs, Incas and Mayans at a Stable they do not have.

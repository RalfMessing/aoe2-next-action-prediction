### 8.0 Camel hypothesis

The tree gives `Camel Scout` (Unit 1755, train time 22 s) to Gurjaras only. In the log, 237 sessions queue it and 30 start with one. Scenario C counts civs with either unit. This is not confirmed and needs a decision for `manual_mapping.csv`.

It is a pointer, not proof of a single cause: the tree may have changed since 2023, a player may have converted a building, or the log may mislabel more units.
